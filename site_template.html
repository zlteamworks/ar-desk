How the odds are scored

A weighted heuristic, not a prediction. It favours roles where a real employer is actively screening right now — those convert. It is honest about what it cannot see: undisclosed salary is marked as estimated, never implied to be in band.

Weighting

Where these come from

Naukri and LinkedIn job boards, plus employers' own Workday systems — the Workday rows are direct requisitions with no agency in between.

Salary bands are marked Stated when the employer published a range and Est. when it was inferred from the experience band. Always verify CTC before investing time in an application.
