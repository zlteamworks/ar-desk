For hiring teams

Help the right candidates reach you.

TalentTap surfaces fresh finance openings and makes requirements easier to understand. Add a public recruiting email or phone number to the original job posting and it can appear here as a source-posted contact on the next refresh.

Clear requirements and salary details improve candidate decisions. Employer-direct roles receive a visible trust signal. Candidate resumes remain private on their own devices.

How listing priority works

Listing priority is a sorting aid based on employer verification, posting freshness and reported applicant counts. It is not an interview probability. Your age, salary and experience do not change it. Resume match measures detected text overlap, not capability or eligibility. Unpublished salary stays unknown.
